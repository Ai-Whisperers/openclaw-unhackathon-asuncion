## 3. ORGANIZATIONAL MODEL

### Structure
- Centralized platform + brand control (HQ sets rules, format, screening criteria)
- Decentralized execution (local organizers run events independently)
- City organizers are ideally "technical founders at early AI startups"
- Organizers follow City Organizer's Quickstart Guide (standardized tradecraft)

### Revenue Model
- Claims "not monetized" - only sponsor funding covers venue + food/drink
- Sponsorship tiers exist with detailed deliverables (see sponsor analysis)
- No public pricing = opaque financial flows
