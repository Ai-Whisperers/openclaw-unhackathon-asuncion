# Sponsor Intelligence Value Assessment

**Date of Analysis:** 2026-02-28

---

## WHAT EACH SPONSOR TIER ACTUALLY BUYS

The sponsorship tiers aren't just marketing packages - they're access levels to human and technical intelligence. Below is each tier reframed through an intelligence lens.

---

## CORE MEETUP SPONSORSHIP

### Main Sponsor
| Official Benefit | Intelligence Translation |
|---|---|
| 2-minute speaking slot | Elicitation opportunity (frame questions for audience) |
| Logo placement | Presence normalization (reduces suspicion of monitoring) |
| Post-event email inclusion | Access to full attendee contact list indirectly |
| 2 tickets | Insert 2 officers/analysts into the room |

### Venue Sponsor
| Official Benefit | Intelligence Translation |
|---|---|
| Provides space and AV | **Controls physical environment, cameras, WiFi, recording infrastructure** |
| Branded item placement | Physical proximity to all attendees |
| Entrance logo | First touchpoint = first impression control |

### Technical Walkthrough
| Official Benefit | Intelligence Translation |
|---|---|
| 5-7 minute walkthrough | Live demonstration of capabilities = both give and take intel |
| QR code for opt-in leads | Self-selecting interest identification |
| Organizer intros | Social engineering warm handoff |

|---|---|

## VIP DINNERS (10-20 builders) - CULTIVATION FORMAT

### Headline Sponsor
| Official Benefit | Intelligence Translation |
|---|---|
| **Venue selection** | **Control physical environment entirely** |
| **Attendee curation collaboration** | **SELECT YOUR TARGETS** |
| 10-minute speaking/presentation slot | Extended elicitation opportunity |
| Discussion moderation option | **Control information flow and conversation direction** |
| Branded gifts | Establish gift-giving/reciprocity dynamic |
| Opt-in QR collection | Identify responsive targets |
| 4 tickets | Insert cultivation team |

**Assessment:** The VIP Dinner format is structurally identical to a classic intelligence cultivation event: small group, controlled environment, curated attendees, gift-giving, managed conversation, and relationship-building infrastructure.

## KEY FINDING

The sponsorship structure creates a **tiered intelligence access model**:

| Access Level | Cost (Estimated) | Intelligence Capability |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter ad | $ | Audience analytics, narrative influence |
| Meetup sponsor | $$ | Room access, 2 analysts, post-event email reach |
| Hackathon marquee | $$$ | Project code access, judging seats, participant profiling |
| VIP dinner headline | $$$$ | Target selection, environment control, cultivation |
| Workshop provider | $$$$$ | Extended access, dependency creation, persistent community channel |

**The price of each tier scales with the intelligence access it provides.** This may be incidental to the business model, but the capability is structural.
